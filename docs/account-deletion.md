# Delete your FriendFlix account

*Deutsch: [de/account-deletion.md](de/account-deletion.md)*

App: FriendFlix for Android (`app.friendflix.android`), published by Manuel Weißböck.

Every FriendFlix server is run by its own operator (you, your household or a friend). Your account and its data live **only on that server**. The publisher of the app has no account of yours and holds no data about you, so the deletion is done on your server.

## Delete it yourself (in the app or in a browser)

1. Open FriendFlix, in the app or at your server's address in a browser, with your **main profile**.
2. Go to **My space → Account**.
3. Optional: **Your data → Download my data** saves a copy of your data as a JSON file.
4. **Delete account…**, then sign in again to confirm.

At once, your account is locked, you are signed out on every device and your downloads stop working. For **7 days** you can undo it by signing in again and choosing to restore the account. After that the deletion is final.

## Ask the operator

If you can no longer sign in, ask the person who runs your FriendFlix server (who invited you) to delete your account. They do it under *Admin → Users*.

## What is deleted, what stays

- **Deleted:** your account and profiles, lists, watchlist, ratings and reviews, watch history, requests, devices, notification settings, and your users on the server's media server and request service.
- **Kept, without your name:** the server's audit log keeps its entries with a pseudonym (`deleted-…`) instead of your name, e-mail and IP address, plus a record that a deletion took place. The sign-in account is deactivated; whether it is deleted too is up to the operator.
- **Backups and logs** of the server follow the operator's own retention.

## Data on your phone

**Settings → Sign out of this device** in the app, or uninstalling it, deletes everything the app stored on the phone: downloads and their keys, cookies and settings. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
